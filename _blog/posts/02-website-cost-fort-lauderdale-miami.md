# What Does a Website Cost in Fort Lauderdale & Miami in 2026?

**Short answer:** in South Florida in 2026, a DIY or AI-builder site runs about $20–$60 per month, a freelancer build lands between $1,500 and $6,000, a small studio build runs roughly $5,000 to $20,000, and a traditional agency engagement starts around $25,000 and climbs fast. Most Miami and Fort Lauderdale small businesses that need the site to actually generate leads land in the studio range, and the number is driven by page count, custom functionality, and whether brand identity is part of the job.

I publish this because the industry's refusal to talk about money wastes everyone's time. Here's the whole picture.

## The four tiers, honestly

| Tier | 2026 price | Timeline | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI builder / DIY | $20–$60 per month | Hours to days | Testing an idea, under 10 pages, no integrations |
| Freelancer | $1,500–$6,000 | 3–8 weeks | Straightforward marketing sites, tight budgets |
| Independent studio | $5,000–$20,000 | 1–4 weeks | Revenue-critical sites, premium positioning, brand + web together |
| Full agency | $25,000+ | 8–20 weeks | Enterprise, multi-stakeholder, large content operations |

Two notes on that table. First, the timelines used to correlate with price: more money, more weeks. AI-native workflows broke that link, which is why the studio row is both cheaper and faster than the agency row. Second, "freelancer" and "studio" overlap heavily; the difference is usually whether strategy and brand are included or whether you're handing over a spec and getting it executed.

## What actually moves the number

Nine times out of ten, it's one of these five:

**Page count and template variety.** Five unique layouts cost more than a fifteen-page site built from four templates. Ask about layouts, not pages.

**Custom functionality.** A contact form is free. Booking with availability logic, a quote calculator, gated resources, CRM routing, a members area, e-commerce: each of those is its own small project.

**Whether brand identity is included.** If you need type and color language, logo work, and guidelines before anyone can design a page, that's a separate craft and a separate line item. Bundling it usually costs less than sequencing it.

**Copywriting.** The quiet budget-killer. Designers design; most don't write your service descriptions unless you pay for it. If you don't have copy and don't plan to write it, price that in, or you'll stall for a month at 80% done.

**Content and asset production.** Photography, video, 3D, illustration. In a market like Miami where hospitality and real estate live or die on imagery, this is often the largest single cost after the build.

## What doesn't justify a higher price

- **Account managers.** You're paying for a telephone game between you and whoever actually does the work.
- **Discovery theater.** Multi-week research phases that produce a deck instead of a website. A focused call plus a clear scope gets you to the same place.
- **Retainers with vague deliverables.** If you can't name what arrives each month, you're funding someone's forecast.
- **"Custom CMS."** In 2026 this usually means proprietary lock-in. Ask what happens if you leave.

## Ongoing costs people forget

Budget roughly $100–$600 a year for domain and hosting on a static or lightly-managed site, more if you're on a platform subscription. Then decide about maintenance: a site that never changes is a site that slowly stops ranking. Whether that's a small monthly arrangement or a quarterly refresh, plan for *something*.

Worth saying plainly: your website is not a one-time purchase, it's a channel. Businesses that treat it as a capital expense every five years always end up paying more than the ones who spend a little continuously.

1. **What's fixed and what's variable?** If the answer is "depends on hours," you don't have a price, you have a starting bid.

## FAQ

**How much does a small business website cost in Miami?**
Most Miami small businesses spend between $5,000 and $20,000 for a professionally designed, conversion-focused site in 2026. Under $2,000 generally means a template build or a freelancer executing a spec you supply; over $25,000 generally means agency overhead.

**Is a cheap website worth it?**
It depends entirely on whether the site has a job. If nobody makes a buying decision on it, cheap is correct. If it's how clients judge whether you're worth a premium rate, a cheap site is the most expensive thing you own.
