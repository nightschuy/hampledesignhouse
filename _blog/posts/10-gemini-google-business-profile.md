# Gemini Can Now Manage Your Google Business Profile. Should You?

**Short answer:** yes, connect Gemini to your Google Business Profile if you run a single location, but use it as an assistant, not an autopilot. It can update hours, draft review replies, schedule posts, and summarize your performance data in plain English. It can't fix a weak website or write replies that sound like you without editing. At Hample Design House in Fort Lauderdale, I build websites that this kind of AI reads from, so here's how I'd set it up.

Google first announced the Business Profile connection in June and made it part of a bigger small business push on October 20. If you own a restaurant, a clinic, a salon, or a contracting business anywhere from Homestead to Jupiter, this is the most practical AI update of the month. It's free, it touches the listing that drives your calls and direction requests, and it takes a few minutes to connect.

## What did Google announce for small businesses in Gemini?

On October 20, 2026, Google published [New Gemini tools for small business owners](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/new-tools-small-business-owners/). The short version:

1. **A business landing page inside the Gemini app**, meant as one place to handle branding, finances, and customer insights.
2. **Connected apps.** Gemini can link to Google Business Profile, Google Ads, and Google Workspace, plus Adobe, Canva, Dropbox, Shopify, Wix, Zoho, and Xero.
3. **On-brand visuals.** You can give it your website, brand kit, fonts, or colors, and it keeps that style when it generates images.
4. **Financial charts** built from spreadsheets, receipts, and purchase records.

Google says it's rolling out globally to all Gemini app users "in the coming days." The Business Profile connection itself was first announced in June (as [PPC Land covered at the time](https://ppc.land/gemini-now-manages-your-google-business-profile-with-a-single-tap/)), and it's the part that matters most for a local business.

Separately, Google is testing a new dashboard called **Google Business Home** that pulls your Business Profile, Merchant Center, YouTube, and Ads accounts into one place. [Search Engine Roundtable reported it on October 22](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-business-home-42363.html); it's a closed beta for select business categories in the U.S. and U.K. The direction is obvious. Google wants you managing your local presence by talking to an AI.

## What can Gemini actually do with my Google Business Profile?

According to [Google's help page for the Gemini connection](https://support.google.com/business/answer/17142585?hl=en), once connected Gemini can:

- **Update your profile:** hours, food menus, photos, attributes, contact info, action links (ordering, reservations, appointments), and location details.
- **Handle reviews:** read ratings and reviews, draft or delete replies, and summarize recurring themes.
- **Post for you:** create, schedule, publish, or delete Update, Offer, and Event posts.
- **Explain your numbers:** analyze impressions, website clicks, direction requests, bookings, and the search terms people used to find you.

It can also build a "business notebook" that combines your profile data with your website content and suggests what to do next, like flagging unanswered questions or missing holiday hours.

That last part is worth pausing on. Gemini reads your website to understand your business. If your site is thin, vague, or out of date, the suggestions will be too.

## Who can connect Gemini to a Business Profile right now?

The requirements are narrower than the announcement suggests. Per Google's help page, you need to:

1. Be the owner or manager of **only one** verified Business Profile.
2. Be 18 or older.
3. Sign in at **gemini.google.com** with a **personal** Google Account linked to the profile. Work and school accounts don't work yet, and it's web only.
4. Have **Keep Activity** turned on, which means your Gemini chats are saved to your account. Read Google's Gemini privacy notice before you connect, especially if you discuss customers by name.

It isn't available in the EEA or the U.K., and it works in a set list of languages that includes English and Spanish. For a bilingual business in Miami-Dade, that's a real plus.

The single-profile rule is the big catch. A restaurant group with three locations, or an agency managing listings for clients, is out for now.

**How to connect:** go to gemini.google.com, ask a question about your business profile, and if Gemini doesn't pick it up, add "@Google Business Profile" to your prompt. Approve the connection when asked. You can disconnect any time from gemini.google.com/apps.

## Should I let AI reply to my Google reviews?

Let it draft. Don't let it publish unread.

Replies matter more than most owners think. In [BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/local-consumer-review-survey/) of 1,002 U.S. consumers, **89%** expect business owners to respond to reviews, and **80%** say they're likely to use a business that responds to all of them. But **50%** say generic or templated replies put them off.

That's the tension. AI makes it easy to answer every review, and just as easy to answer every review with the same polite paste. A customer who wrote three sentences about your server by name doesn't want "Thank you for your feedback, we hope to see you again soon."

My rule of thumb: Gemini writes the first draft, you add one specific detail it couldn't know, then you post. Thirty seconds per review. And never let it touch a negative review without you reading it twice.

## Which Business Profile tasks should I hand to Gemini?

Here's how I'd split the work for a typical single-location business:

| Task | Let Gemini do it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Update regular and holiday hours | Yes | Fast, low risk, and Google may warn customers your hours "might differ" on holidays if you skip it |
| Draft replies to positive reviews | Yes, then edit | Saves time; add one specific detail before posting |
| Reply to negative reviews | Draft only | Tone and facts matter too much to automate |
| Weekly Offer or Event posts | Yes, then edit | Good for seasonal promos; check dates and prices |
| Summarize performance and search terms | Yes | This is where it shines; it turns a dashboard into sentences |
| Change your business category or address | No | These define where and how you show up; make those calls yourself |
| Write your services and business description | Draft only | It needs your real differentiators, which usually live on your website |

The holiday hours line is easy money. [Google's holidays help page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6333474?hl=en) says it may show a warning that your hours might differ on listed holidays, and setting special hours in advance prevents that. With Thanksgiving and the December rush coming, ask Gemini to set them now.

Indirectly, yes. Your Business Profile and your website are the two sources Google trusts most about you. A profile that's current, answered, and active gives Google's AI answers fresher facts to quote. Whatever assistant they use, it's assembling an answer from your listing, your reviews, and your site. Gemini just made one of those three easier to keep clean.

## What can't Gemini do for my business?

A few honest limits:

- **It can't fix your website.** It reads your site to understand you. If the site doesn't clearly say what you do, where, and for whom, Gemini (and every other AI) has less to work with.
- **It can't answer your leads.** Profile messages and calls still need a human or a separate system. If speed is the problem, start with [AI lead response](/blog/ai-lead-response-small-business-florida/).
- **It can't decide your positioning.** It will happily write a business description. Whether that description makes you sound like everyone else on Las Olas is your call.
- **It doesn't replace a strategy.** Posting more isn't a plan. Posting the offer that matches what people are actually searching for is.

## FAQ

**Is Gemini's Google Business Profile connection free?**
Google describes the new small business tools as rolling out to all Gemini app users and hasn't announced a separate price for the Business Profile connection. You sign in at gemini.google.com with the personal Google Account that manages your profile.

**Can I connect Gemini if I manage more than one location?**
Not yet. Google's help page says the feature isn't available if you manage more than one verified Business Profile, which rules out most multi-location businesses and agencies for now.

**Can Gemini post review replies without my approval?**
Gemini can draft and post replies when you ask it to, so the safe habit is to read and edit every draft before it goes live. Consumers notice templated replies, and half say generic responses put them off.

**Does Gemini work in Spanish for my Business Profile?**
Yes. Spanish is on Google's list of supported languages for the Business Profile connection, along with English and about ten others.

**Will connecting Gemini improve my Google ranking?**
Not directly. It makes it easier to keep hours, posts, and review replies current, and an accurate, active profile gives Google's search and AI answers better information about your business.
