# Google Is Putting Ads in AI Mode. Should Your Small Business Pay?

**Short answer:** ads in Google AI Mode are worth testing for a local business only after your landing page, call tracking, and conversion tracking are solid, because the ad buys the click and your website does the rest. Google announced on October 28 that it is scaling AI Mode ads and adding multiturn ads in the U.S. At Hample Design House in Fort Lauderdale, I build the websites and landing pages those ads point to, so here's how I'd decide.

Short version of the long version: AI Mode ads are not a new channel you need to master. They are mostly your existing Google Ads campaigns showing up in a new place. The real question is whether your campaigns and your site are ready for it.

## What did Google announce about ads in AI Mode?

On October 28, 2026, Google's VP of Search Ads, Shashi Thakur, [announced the expansion on Google's blog](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/). The parts that matter for a U.S. business:

1. **Multiturn ads in the U.S.** Ads can now appear on answers to follow-up questions inside an AI Mode conversation, not just the first query.
2. **More countries.** Ads in AI Mode are scaling in Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines, and Singapore, with Brazil, Mexico, and the U.K. planned for the fourth quarter.
3. **Clear labels.** Google says ads will be labeled "Sponsored."
4. **Longer queries.** Google says AI Mode queries are on average three times longer than traditional searches.
5. **Comparable clicks.** Google says click-through rates are already similar between sponsored and organic product listings inside AI Mode. It did not publish the numbers.

This isn't brand new. Google first put ads in AI Mode in May 2025, as [Search Engine Roundtable noted in its coverage](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-expands-ads-ai-mode-42412.html). What's new is the scale and the follow-up placement.

## Why is Google pushing ads into AI Mode now?

Money. On Alphabet's October 28 earnings call, Google's chief business officer Philipp Schindler said AI Mode has "lower monetization" than traditional Search, [as 9to5Google reported](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/29/google-stock-drop-ai-mode-ad-expansion/). People are using AI Mode more, and Google earns less per query there. More ad inventory is the obvious fix.

That matters to you for one reason: expect more ad space in AI answers over the next year, not less. Organic space in an AI answer is already tight. If paid placements grow, the free spots get tighter.

## How do ads get into Google AI Mode?

You don't buy "AI Mode ads" as a separate product. Your regular campaigns become eligible. Google's Ads Liaison, Ginny Marvin, laid out the rules in July, [as summarized by TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/industry-news/google-ai-search-eligibility-qualified-future-conversions-creator-partnerships-2026):

- **Search campaigns with broad match keywords and Smart Bidding** are eligible.
- **Keywordless campaigns** are eligible: AI Max for Search, Performance Max, Shopping, and Dynamic Search Ads.
- **Exact and phrase match only** campaigns were not eligible. Google started [a small test in September](https://www.techwyse.com/?p=78228) that lets some of them show text ads in AI Mode for queries with clear intent.

So if you already run a broad match or Performance Max campaign, there's a decent chance you're already showing in AI Mode. The catch: Google doesn't give you a clean, separate report for AI Mode performance, so you're trusting that the blended results still hold up.

## Should a small business pay for ads in AI Mode?

It depends less on AI Mode and more on whether paid search already works for you.

| Your situation | What I'd do |
|---|---|
| You don't run Google Ads yet | Don't start because of AI Mode. Fix your site and Business Profile first, then test a small Search campaign |
| You run Search ads and track calls and form fills | Test broad match with Smart Bidding or AI Max on one service, with a set budget and a 60-day check |
| You run ads but can't say what a lead costs you | Stop adding placements. Set up conversion tracking before spending another dollar |
| You sell products online | Shopping and Performance Max already reach AI Mode. Make sure your product feed and product pages are complete |
| You're booked solid from referrals | Skip it. Put the effort into reviews and an accurate website |

AI Mode queries being three times longer cuts both ways. A longer query ("emergency AC repair in Plantation that can come tonight") tells you a lot about intent, which is good. It also means Google, not you, decides which longer questions match your campaign, which is why tracking matters more here than in classic keyword campaigns.

## What should be in place before you pay for AI Mode ads?

Run through this before you raise a budget:

1. **A dedicated landing page per service.** Not your homepage. One page, one offer, one action. If the ad says "same-day AC repair," the page says it too.
2. **One-tap contact on mobile.** Call and book buttons visible on the first screen, because the person tapping an ad is often standing in a parking lot.
3. **Conversion tracking that counts real leads.** Form submissions, calls longer than 60 seconds, and bookings. Not page views.
4. **Call tracking.** A tracked number on the landing page so you can tie phone leads to campaigns.
5. **Fast load time.** Paid traffic is expensive traffic. A slow page wastes it before anyone reads a word.
6. **A plan to answer leads fast.** Paid leads go cold fast, and you paid for every one. I covered this in [where AI actually pays first for small businesses](/blog/ai-lead-response-small-business-florida/).
7. **Matching facts everywhere.** Prices, hours, and service areas on the landing page should match your Business Profile and your ad copy. AI answers notice contradictions.
8. **A budget cap and a review date.** Pick a number you're fine losing as a test, and a date to judge it.

If you can't check items 3 and 4, you can't tell whether AI Mode ads work for you. You'll just have a bill.

## FAQ

**Can I buy ads only in Google AI Mode?**
Not as a separate product. Ads reach AI Mode through campaigns that are already eligible, mainly broad match Search with Smart Bidding, AI Max for Search, Performance Max, Shopping, and Dynamic Search Ads.

**What are multiturn ads in Google AI Mode?**
They are ads that appear on Google's answers to follow-up questions within an AI Mode conversation. Google announced them for the U.S. on October 28, 2026.

**Are ads in AI Mode labeled?**
Yes. Google says ads in AI Mode are clearly labeled "Sponsored," the same as elsewhere in Search.

**How much should a small business budget to test Google AI Mode ads?**
Enough to get a meaningful number of clicks in your industry. With national median costs between about $2 and $10 per click, a $1,000 to $2,000 monthly test for 60 days gives most local service businesses enough data to judge.

**Will ads in AI Mode push my free listing down?**
Possibly. Google is adding ad inventory to AI answers, which leaves less room for unpaid links. A clear, accurate website and a strong Business Profile are still the best way to hold your organic spot.
