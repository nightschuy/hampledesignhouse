# Bluehost AI Builder: Should a Small Business Build Its Own Site?

**Short answer:** Bluehost AI Builder, launched November 10, 2026, builds websites and simple web apps (booking, quote requests, customer portals) from a plain-English chat, free to try and $20 to $80 a month for paid plans. It's a fair pick for testing an idea. For a business that needs to be found on Google and in AI answers, check its search settings first. At Hample Design House in Fort Lauderdale, I'd use it to prototype, then build the real site.

Here's what it does, what it costs, what the first hands-on testers found, and how to decide.

## What is Bluehost AI Builder?

Bluehost [announced AI Builder on November 10](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluehost-launches-ai-builder-turning-business-ideas-into-websites-and-working-apps-in-minutes-302935303.html) at its first small business summit. You describe what you want in a chat, answer a few follow-up questions, and it builds a site or a working app, then publishes it to Bluehost hosting in one click.

The interesting part isn't the website. AI website builders have been around for a while (I compared them to hiring a designer in [AI website builder vs. hiring a designer](/blog/ai-website-builder-vs-designer/)). The interesting part is the apps. According to [Bluehost's own launch post](https://www.bluehost.com/blog/bluehost-ai-builder-launch/), it can build:

- **Websites:** business sites, portfolios, landing pages, and blogs.
- **Web apps:** appointment booking, quote requests, customer portals, directories, and internal workflow tools.
- **A back office:** saved form submissions, user accounts with roles, an admin panel, email alerts, and bookings that connect to Google Calendar.

Bluehost also says it generates page titles, descriptions, headings, and image alt text automatically. One caveat it states plainly: these are web apps that open in a browser, not native apps in the App Store or Google Play.

That's a real shift. A year ago, a Coral Springs pool service wanting online quote requests with a customer login was looking at a custom build or a stack of subscriptions. Now a chat window will take a swing at it.

## How much does Bluehost AI Builder cost?

From [Bluehost's AI Builder page](https://www.bluehost.com/ai-builder) and [Gaurav Tiwari's hands-on review](https://gauravtiwari.org/bluehost-ai-builder-review/) (published November 10):

| Plan | Monthly price | AI credits | Custom domain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 5 a day, up to 30 a month | No (bluehost.ai subdomain) |
| Lite | $20 | 100 a month | Yes |
| Standard | $40 | 200 a month | Yes |
| Pro | $80 (per Tiwari's review) | 400 a month | Yes |

Credits pay for building and editing. Hosting doesn't use credits, so a published site stays up if you stop editing. Credits expire after 30 days. Tiwari reports annual billing brings each paid tier down about 20%, and top-up packs start at 50 credits for $15. Your domain registration is extra.

For scale: Tiwari's test bakery site took 4 credits for a first build, a headline edit, a booking page, and a deposit step. So the credit budget is generous for a small site. The real cost is the monthly fee for as long as you stay, plus your time.

## What did early testers find?

Tiwari's review is the most detailed test I've found so far, and it's worth reading in full before you commit. A few findings stood out to me, all specific to his November test, and any of them may change quickly:

1. **The free address is hidden from Google.** He found the free bluehost.ai subdomain sends a noindex header, so Google won't list it. He couldn't confirm whether a paid custom domain removes it.
2. **AI search crawlers were blocked by default.** The default robots.txt on his test blocked GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, and others.
3. **Common SEO basics were missing.** No canonical tag, Open Graph tags, local business schema, favicon, or sitemap on his build.
4. **No code export.** He found no way to download the site. Your site and its data live inside AI Builder.
5. **Speed was decent, not great.** Mobile PageSpeed 84, desktop 93, with the largest image taking 4.4 seconds on mobile.
6. **The builder made choices on its own.** On the booking page, it picked the booking rules without asking.

None of that is a scandal. It's a brand-new product, and Bluehost will likely fix some of it. But item 2 is the one I'd check first. If AI crawlers can't read your site, ChatGPT and Perplexity are describing you from other people's pages. With ChatGPT now letting businesses claim their own profile, that's a bad week to be invisible to it. ## When does Bluehost AI Builder make sense?

I'll be fair to it. There are good uses:

- **Testing an idea before you spend real money.** A side business, a new service line, a pop-up. Build it free, show it to ten customers, see if anyone books.
- **Internal tools nobody searches for.** A job intake form for your crew, a simple directory for your team, a client portal for a handful of existing customers. Search visibility doesn't matter there.
- **A placeholder while the real site gets built.** Something live beats nothing, as long as you know it's temporary.
- **Very tight budgets.** At $20 a month, it's cheaper than almost anything for a basic online presence.

## When should you hire a designer instead?

The trade-offs show up when the site's job is to bring in new customers:

1. **You need to be found.** If local search and AI answers are where your leads come from, you need full control of indexing, crawler access, schema, and sitemaps. That's the foundation from [how to get linked in Google AI Mode](/blog/google-ai-mode-website-links/).
2. **You want to own it.** No export means switching later is a rebuild. A site you own can move hosts in an afternoon.
3. **Your brand has to stand out.** Every AI builder pulls toward the same safe layouts. If you're competing with five other med spas in Boca, looking like a template costs you.
4. **The math over two years.** At $40 a month, you'll spend about $960 over two years and still not own the result. A flat-price site often costs more upfront and less over its life. [Here's what a website costs in Fort Lauderdale and Miami](/blog/website-cost-fort-lauderdale-miami/).

My actual recommendation is a hybrid. Use a tool like this to sketch what you want: the pages, the booking flow, the questions your customers ask. Then bring that sketch to a designer. You'll save hours of discovery, and you'll know exactly what you're paying for. That's how I work anyway: AI for speed, a person accountable for the result, live in about a week through [/ship/](/ship/).

## Before you start with any AI builder: a 5-point check

1. **Search settings:** after publishing, look for a noindex tag or header, and open yoursite.com/robots.txt to see which crawlers are blocked.
2. **Domain:** connect a domain you own, not a subdomain, before you print it on anything.
3. **Exit plan:** find out if you can export your pages and your form data. If not, keep your own copies.
4. **Booking rules:** read every rule the AI set for deposits, cancellations, and hours. It may have guessed.
5. **Tracking:** confirm you can count calls, form submissions, and bookings, so you know if the site is working.

## FAQ

**Is Bluehost AI Builder free?**
There's a free plan with 5 AI credits a day, up to 30 a month, that publishes on a bluehost.ai subdomain. A custom domain requires a paid plan starting at $20 a month.

**Can Bluehost AI Builder make a booking app for my business?**
Yes. Bluehost says it can build appointment booking, quote requests, customer portals, and directories that run in a mobile browser. It doesn't publish native apps to the Apple App Store or Google Play.

**Is a Bluehost AI Builder site good for SEO?**
It generates titles, descriptions, and alt text, but an early hands-on review found the free address was set to noindex and AI crawlers were blocked by default. Check those settings before relying on it for search traffic.

**Can I move my Bluehost AI Builder site somewhere else later?**
An early review found no option to export the site's code. Plan on rebuilding if you outgrow it, and keep your own copies of content and form data.

**Should I use an AI builder or hire a web designer in South Florida?**
Use an AI builder to test an idea or for internal tools. Hire a designer when the site needs to win new customers through search and stand out, and when you want to own what you paid for.
